# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

People encountering Ac through coaching, CrossFit, business, software, or Field Notes. They are deciding who he is, whether his work is credible, and whether to read, collaborate, or ask about coaching. Most arrive casually on a phone and should understand the site before investing attention.

## Product Purpose

accoworks.dev is Ac's personal operating record and public front door. It connects his work as a CrossFit gym owner, CrossFit Philippines Country Manager, coach, business operator, and builder. Success means a visitor quickly understands the breadth of the work without mistaking it for an agency or generic developer portfolio, then chooses a clear next path: projects, Field Notes, or coaching.

## Brand Personality

Grounded, capable, and field-tested. The voice is direct, specific, practical, and human. It should feel like documentation from someone who runs real operations, not commentary from the sidelines.

## Anti-references

Do not resemble an AI-generated SaaS landing page or generic developer portfolio. Avoid animated gradient text, decorative particles, glass effects, glowing card grids, terminal motifs used as costume, excessive pills, repetitive equal cards, and claims without evidence. Do not drift into corporate consulting language or polished-but-empty personal branding.

## Design Principles

1. Lead with the operator, not the effects.
2. Show evidence before making claims.
3. Let different kinds of work use different editorial structures.
4. Keep interactions honest: interactive surfaces behave interactively, static content stays quiet.
5. Make the first mobile viewport explain the site.
6. Preserve Ac's direct writing and concrete Cebu context.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA. Maintain strong keyboard navigation, visible focus, readable contrast, reduced-motion support, semantic structure, useful alternative text, and comfortable mobile touch targets. The site must remain understandable without animation or client-side JavaScript.
