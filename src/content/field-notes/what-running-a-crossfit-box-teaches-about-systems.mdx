---
title: "What Running a CrossFit Box Teaches About Systems"
date: "2026-05-30"
label: "Building"
summary: "A gym looks like energy from the outside. From the inside, it survives on systems people can actually follow."
---

A CrossFit box looks like noise from the outside.

Music. Barbells. Chalk. People breathing hard. Coaches moving around the floor. Classes turning over every hour.

But underneath that noise, the business only works when the systems are clear enough that people can trust them without thinking too hard.

The class has to start on time. The coach has to know the intent. The equipment has to be where people expect it. New members have to know what happens next. The staff needs a shared standard for what good looks like.

If those things are unclear, the energy leaks out of the room.

That is the part outsiders miss. Vibe is not the opposite of structure. Vibe is often the result of structure that people no longer notice because it works.

The best systems in a gym are not complicated. They are obvious, repeatable, and hard to misunderstand. They let the coach focus on people instead of logistics.

I want more of my businesses to work that way.

Not more software for the sake of software. Not dashboards because dashboards look serious. Just cleaner defaults, better handoffs, and fewer moments where a good person has to guess what the system should have already made clear.
