I have been seeing more local business ads that look AI-generated.

Too glossy. Too smooth. Strange lighting. People who almost look real. Captions that sound polished, but say nothing.

The business may be good.

The product may be good.

But the ad makes it feel cheap.

I use AI every day, so this is not me rejecting the tool. I use it for writing, coding, research, planning, admin, and sorting through messy ideas.

It helps me move faster.

But AI still needs taste around it.

Good AI should be like good CGI.

When CGI is done well, you do not notice it. You stay inside the story.

Bad CGI pulls you out.

Once you notice the shortcut, the trust breaks.

AI works the same way.

Use it for the boring parts.

Inventory. Inquiries. SOPs. Admin. Research. Voice notes. Planning.

Let it remove friction.

But be careful when it touches the parts of the business people actually see.

The ad still needs taste.

The photo still needs intention.

The caption still needs a human voice.

The design still needs someone who understands what the business should feel like.

Artists and graphic designers do more than make assets. They notice when something feels off.

Pay them when the work matters.

Use AI for convenience, not as an excuse to stop caring.

Automate the system.

Protect the soul.
