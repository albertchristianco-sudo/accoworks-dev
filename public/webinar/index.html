Why some affiliates grow
and others stall

The business systems that make the difference

AC Co · CF-L3 · Country Manager for Affiliates, Philippines
South Asia Webinar · 10 September 2026

The question

Two gyms. Same city.
Same equipment. Same coaches.

Five years later, one grows and compounds.
The other stalls, and the owner works harder every year just to stay in place.

The difference is not coaching.

It is the systems
around the coaching.

Definition

What is a system?

  • TriggerIt starts on a schedule or an event, not on your memory.
  • OwnerYou, a coach, or a machine runs it the same way every time.
  • VerifyYour job shifts from doing everything to checking it ran.

If it lives only in your head, it is a task.
And you are the bottleneck.

CrossFit Subtero

I was the bottleneck

  • StallMembers quietly leaving. All marketing depended on my bandwidth.
  • SystemHired a marketing manager, from inside the gym.
  • ResultConsistent branding, content ships without me, inquiries keep coming.

My job now is to verify it ran, not to run it myself.

Pillar 1 · Prospecting & Sales

You cannot sell
to "everyone"

"Everyone" is not a person. You can only sell to a person with a specific problem. Prospecting is the job of finding them.

Pillar 1 · What I actually do

Get your name out there

  • EventsCommunity runs, wellness days, expos, school fairs. Every room is a pipeline.
  • Cold emailCompanies with health programs → the HR manager, one by one.

Be aggressive. Nobody is coming to find your gym.

Pillar 1 · West ≠ Asia

The principle travels.
The tactic does not always.

The playbook says

  1. Pick up the phone and call your leads

Our market says

  1. Text first
  2. Instagram message
  3. Email

Same follow-up, different channel. You know your market better than any playbook.

Pillar 1 · The takeaway

The affiliate that grows has a pipeline.

The affiliate that stalls waits for walk-ins.

Pillar 2 · Why it matters

Integration is cheap,
and it compounds

Prospecting costs money to find one member. Integration costs attention to keep one, and that member stays for years, brings referrals, and does your prospecting for you.

Pillar 3 · Service stacks in four levels

From the floor up

  • 1The facility. The 2026 bare minimum. Nothing survives a dirty gym.
  • 2The experience. On time, every time. Boring and consistent builds trust.
  • 3The relationship. Weekly attendance scan → one personal message.
  • 4The recovery. The complaint is a gift. Respond fast and mean it.

The roof · Finances

Passion does not pay rent

Owners who know every snatch PR to the kilo, and not their own monthly costs. Knowing your numbers is not advanced strategy. It is the most basic rule of any business.

The roof · Your first homework

Three numbers + one hour

1

Cost per member

Everything it costs, divided by members.

2

Revenue per member

The real average, not the sticker price.

3

Cash runway

Months you survive if revenue stops.

The monthly money hour: one hour, same day every month. A notebook is enough. One hour a month, and your business stops being a mystery.

The AI-ready affiliate

AI is not the future.

It is the present.

Your members use it. Your leads use it. The traveler looking for a drop-in is using it today. The only choice left is whether your gym shows up in it.

Part 1 · Be in the AI's answer

"Best CrossFit gyms
in my area?"

  • 01Google reviews. Real, steady, replied-to. Ask on milestone days.
  • 02Website + Business Profile. Schedule, prices, hours, drop-ins, in plain text, current.
  • 03Organic talk. Reddit and forums. Earned and invited, never faked.
The one warning

Do not try to game ChatGPT.

Become the most verifiable
answer on the web.

Part 2 · Let AI run the systems

The machine drafts.
You verify.

  • SalesFollow-ups drafted, outreach personalized, pipeline stops slipping.
  • MembersDay-zero, week-one, month-one messages on schedule. Quiet members flagged.
  • AdminBreakdowns and content drafted, so your hours go to the floor.

And do not get lazy: AI should make you more consistent, never more generic.
