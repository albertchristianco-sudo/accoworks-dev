Digital Operations Center · Phase 1 Build Brief

Operations,
made visible.

Phase 1 of the Techisland Digital Operations Center. Job orders, daily AI assignments, field reports, and self-calculating KPIs for the Implementation, Support, and R&D teams.

Prepared for Ryan Yu Fine Upgrades & Techisland June 2026

Today, manpower visibility lives in people's heads and chat threads. Phase 1 turns it into a system you can see and measure from the Friday meeting to the daily report.

01

Friday meeting

Salde and the department heads review every active project and assign the coming week's work.

02

Job orders

Brigette creates next week's job orders before Monday: who, travel time, man-hours, task type, project.

03

AI assessment

Each morning the AI reviews open work and skills, then proposes the optimal daily allocation per person.

04

Execution

Field staff work the approved assignment, checking in on site from their phones.

05

EOD report

Every field employee files an end-of-day report: time per device, work done, delays, photos and video.

06

KPI scoring

Actual hours measure themselves against the estimate. KPIs compute automatically, no manual tallies.

07

Dashboard

Live project status, team KPI scores, the support queue, R&D progress, and flagged delays in one place.

The three modules

Where the work lives.

Phase 1 starts with the teams that control daily execution: Implementation, Support, and R&D. Each is owned by a named lead, with Salde supervising overall.

Implementation

Project Implementation

Abigail Sadia leads · Salde oversees
  • Weekly job orders created by Brigette after the Friday meeting, live before Monday.
  • Each line carries the employee(s) assigned and expected travel time.
  • Expected man-hours per task and device, auto-filled from the standard estimates.
  • Task type and project reference on every line.
Support

Support Desk

Junmar Cagurol leads
  • Junmar enters support requirements daily or weekly into a single queue.
  • Items are assigned to support staff with response and resolution timers.
  • Covers client support tickets and internal office or showroom needs.
  • Built so KPIs (response time, resolution, escalation) score themselves.
R&D

R&D / Engineering

Salde Carawana leads
  • Salde assigns tasks to Julius and the R&D team.
  • Task types: showroom upgrades, new device testing, new product evaluation.
  • Every task carries milestones and an expected completion date.
  • Salde rates progress monthly, feeding the R&D KPI score.
AI Daily Work Assessment

A plan for the day, every morning.

The AI does the morning math so the leads do not have to, then hands them a plan to approve.

1

Reads everything open

All open job orders, support tickets, and R&D tasks, including anything overdue.

2

Checks people

Each employee's role, skills, and availability against what the work requires.

3

Recommends allocation

The optimal daily assignment per person, balancing priority, capacity, and travel.

4

People commit

The plan is a recommendation. Salde and Abigail approve or adjust before it goes live.
