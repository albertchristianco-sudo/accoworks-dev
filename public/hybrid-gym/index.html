Study the market, price the build

Visit the competing gyms and studios as a customer. Price the equipment list through the China channel, book the aircon service assessment, and write the differentiation memo from evidence.

Decide on numbers

Gate passed: lock rent terms, order the lean build, set the open date. Gate failed: walk away, keep the presale list and the brand equity.

The gate: 40+ founding pack buyers, under 30% Underground overlap, rent inside the ₱250-350/sqm expectation. Pass all three and this becomes a build order, not a bet.

Why go
The sport is heading to a 2M+ athlete season with Asia as its growth front. Cebu's feeder base is proven and already raced a HYROX-branded event. The space is beside your own gym, the brand is yours, and the model breaks even near 520 paid sessions a month, about 20 seats a day, on two revenue streams. Nobody else in Cebu holds the campus, the bench, and the APAC line together.
Why the gate is mandatory
Published premium rates in Cebu top out near ₱4,500 a month and ₱800 a session, and the ceiling above is untested. The unit's rent terms are not signed. The gym-going pool is 0.53% of the population, the regional economy cooled in 2025, and a nearby affiliate is chasing the same audience. Deposits, terms, and origin tags settle the unknowns for about ₱150k.
