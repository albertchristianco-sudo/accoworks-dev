Who we are

More than ten years of showing up

CrossFit Subtero has coached Cebu for more than a decade, from two boxes: HQ in Mandaue and Underground, a basement gym at Horizons in Cebu City.

Subtero HQ is an official HYROX Training Club. Underground runs our Hybrid program. Classes cover every level: CrossFit, HYROX, Hybrid, Barbell Club, Gymnastics, Build & Burn and CrossFit Kids.

Our members do not only train together. They travel to race together, and they fill the stands for each other.

Our coaches and coaching culture

Great coaching is a craft

It asks for a lifetime of dedication, so we built a way to keep at it. Subtero runs its own coach development program, something few gyms offer. Coaches grow inside it from their first class onward, and keep sharpening long after.

  • Every class has a coach on the floor.
  • Coaches are developed, not only hired.
  • One Subtero standard at every location, including this one.

The vision

One premium club.
Three ways to train.

  1. Machine gymA complete strength and machine floor for members who train on their own: racks, dumbbells, cable and plate-loaded machines.
  2. Hybrid and HYROXTurf sled lanes, ergs and stations built to race standard, for coached hybrid classes and race simulations.
  3. Some CrossFitA rig and coached classes that carry the Subtero method into the new club.

All of it in a room designed as a destination: a place people want to train in, photograph and come back to.

Why hybrid and HYROX

The race Filipinos are flying out for

  • Hybrid fitness racing is one of the fastest-growing participation sports in the world. One format, the same race in every city.
  • Filipino athletes already travel to race in Singapore, Bangkok and Hong Kong.
  • Reports point to a first Philippine HYROX race. It is widely expected, though not yet confirmed.
  • Hong Kong hosts the HYROX World Championships in 2027, the first ever in Asia-Pacific.
  • Cebu athletes have asked for bigger gyms, standard equipment and local events, so they do not have to leave Cebu to prepare.

Sources: HYROX and PR Newswire (June 2026), SwimBikeRun.ph and What's On Manila on a Philippine race, Daily Tribune on Bangkok, SunStar Cebu (August 2026).

Why 24 hours

Training on their own clock

Modern professionals train when their day allows, not when a front desk opens. The best international clubs answer this with secure member access: keycard or biometric entry, CCTV and a lit, safe floor.

Coached classes run in staffed hours. Member access reaches beyond them.

  1. OpenStaffed hours with coached classes and open gym.
  2. ExtendMember access before and after staffed hours, once building access and security are agreed.
  3. Around the clockFull 24-hour member access, phased in together with QOMPLEX.

Layout concept

Built along the long axis

The unit is a long, clear-span rectangle, which suits hybrid training well. Turf lanes run nearly the full length. The rig uses the tall clear height under the trusses. Members enter into a lounge with a view straight down the room.

  • Reception and lounge
  • Full-length turf sled lanes
  • Rig zone: ropes and wall balls
  • Strength and machine floor
  • Cardio and ergs

Concept only, to be developed with your team after a site visit.
